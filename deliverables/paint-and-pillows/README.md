# Paint & Pillows — logo concepts

Round 1 explores three black-and-white directions for an interior design site. The name is shown in a clean, editorial sans-serif wordmark on white; the supporting line is a temporary category descriptor and can be removed.

## Concepts

- **A · Interlock** — two opposing P forms share one structural rhythm. The monogram feels architectural, ownable, and strong as a favicon.
- **B · Soft Corner** — a rounded cushion/swatch block uses a white folded stroke and dot for a quiet paint-meets-pillow cue. The most direct and instantly legible direction.
- **C · Sweep** — a single gestural line makes an ampersand-like loop, ending in a paint dot. More expressive and studio-led; strongest when the brand wants a creative signature.

**Recommendation: B · Soft Corner.** It is the clearest meeting point between the name and the category: the rounded block feels like a cushion, while the white sweep reads as a paint gesture. It stays distinctive, compact, and legible as a site mark or favicon.

## Assumptions

- Minimal, black-only identity on white for a polished editorial interior-design feel.
- The site needs a horizontal header lockup plus a symbol that can live alone as an avatar or favicon.
- The wordmark shown here is an exploration lockup; after direction approval, the lettering should be tightened and converted to outlined vector paths for production.

Pick one direction, or call out what to combine (for example, **A’s monogram with B’s softer proportions**). The next round can refine the chosen mark, finalize typography, and produce black/white, horizontal, stacked, symbol-only, favicon, and web-ready variants.
